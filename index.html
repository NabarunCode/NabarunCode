01 / ABOUT

I'm not starting from scratch.

I've spent most of my career around systems — Windows servers, virtualization, Azure, cloud infrastructure and the messy reality of production troubleshooting.

That experience taught me something I don't want to leave behind: how to think about systems, failures, dependencies and what happens when things don't behave the way the documentation promised.

Now I'm applying that systems mindset to AI — learning Python, machine learning, deep learning, LLMs, inference, RAG, agents and MLOps.

The goal

Not simply to become someone who knows how to use AI tools. To understand enough of what's underneath them to build useful things.
