01 / ABOUT

I'm not starting from scratch.

For 20 years, my job was keeping systems alive. Windows servers, virtualization, Azure, cloud. The messy reality of production, where problems don't wait until Monday morning.

That work taught me something I'm not leaving behind. How to think in systems. How things break. What really happens when they don't behave the way the docs promised.

Now I point that same instinct at AI. Python, machine learning, deep learning, LLMs, inference, RAG, agents, MLOps. Same brain, new stack.

The goal

Not just to become someone who can use AI tools. I want to understand enough of what's under the hood to build things that actually work.
